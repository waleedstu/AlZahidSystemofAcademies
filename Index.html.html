Questions

Stuff people
always ask.

Is there an entry test?

No. Bring your last report card and a copy of the B-Form, and you can enrol the same day.

What does it cost?

Fee structure is given in writing at the campus, per class and per course. Ask on WhatsApp and we'll send the current sheet.

Can I do school in the morning and ZIC in the evening?

Yes — that's exactly what the evening batches are for. Most ZIC students are Matric and Inter students studying elsewhere during the day.

Do I get a certificate?

Every ZIC course ends with an institute certificate, handed out at the Grand Award Ceremony along with your project portfolio.

Do I need my own laptop?

No. All ZIC sessions run in the campus lab. If you have a laptop you're welcome to bring it.
